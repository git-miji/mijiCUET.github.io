Why this matters for photonics

Eight years of optical network engineering is not a detour from nanophotonics — it is the macroscopic end of the same physics. Loss budgets, dispersion, modal behaviour, coupling efficiency and the gap between a design and its measured performance are daily concerns in both. That experience makes me sceptical of simulations that have not been validated.
