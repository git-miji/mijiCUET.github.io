Kolmogorov–Arnold networks vs. neural networks for system dynamics

A comparative study identifying the dynamics of a third-order linear time-invariant system and a second-order nonlinear system, benchmarking KANs against conventional neural networks on accuracy and interpretability. Implemented in Python with NumPy, SciPy and Matplotlib.

Relevance to photonics — the same surrogate-modelling machinery can learn a device's spectral response from a set of full-wave simulations, replacing expensive sweeps during optimisation.

Weather forecasting from GHCNd climate data

Correlation analysis and predictive modelling on Global Historical Climatology Network daily data. Decision Tree, Linear Regression, Multi-Output Linear Regression and k-Nearest Neighbours were compared across targets.

Results — Decision Tree performed best for snow thickness and snowfall (test R² ≈ 0.93); k-Nearest Neighbours led on average temperature (test R² ≈ 0.87).

Grade 3 Math — practice & test engine

A browser-based practice and testing tool for Grade 3 mathematics, built as part of a PreK–12 curriculum project. 151 procedural generators cover arithmetic, all nine geometry chapters, and Math Olympiad problems in the style of Math Kangaroo and MOEMS, across five difficulty levels. Every question is generated fresh, so no two attempts are alike, and both practice and test modes are timed.

Verification — every olympiad formula is checked against brute-force computation, and the bank is swept for structural errors across all subjects and levels.
