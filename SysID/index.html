Project · System identification

Kolmogorov–Arnold networks vs. neural networks

Identifying the dynamics of a third-order linear and a second-order nonlinear system, comparing accuracy and interpretability between KANs and conventional neural networks.

Goal

Identify the dynamics of linear and nonlinear systems using a Kolmogorov–Arnold Network (KAN), and compare the result against conventional neural-network system identification. The study covers a third-order linear time-invariant system and a second-order nonlinear system.

Approach

Implemented in Python using NumPy, SciPy and Matplotlib. Both model families were trained on simulated state trajectories, then evaluated on their ability to reproduce the true state evolution and to expose interpretable structure.

Why it matters

KANs place learnable activation functions on the edges of the network rather than fixed nonlinearities on the nodes. That makes the learned model far easier to read as a symbolic expression — valuable when the objective is to understand a system, not merely predict it.
