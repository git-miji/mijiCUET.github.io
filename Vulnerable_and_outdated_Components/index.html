Project · Cybersecurity

Vulnerable and outdated components

Assessing the risk created by unpatched and end-of-life software — identification, severity ranking, and a practical mitigation strategy.

Overview

A cybersecurity assessment of the risk created by vulnerable, unpatched and end-of-life software components — one of the most persistent causes of real-world compromise, and a standing entry in the OWASP Top 10.

Scope

  • Identifying vulnerable and outdated components across a software stack
  • Ranking findings by severity and exploitability
  • Dependency and supply-chain considerations
  • Mitigation strategy, patch management and monitoring
